Upload a street level photo taken in Europe. A 4.5M-parameter model works out where it was probably taken and drops a pin on the map, along with its best guess at the country.

Try it

First load can take up to a minute. The demo runs on a free Hugging Face Space that sleeps when nobody has used it for two days. Once it is awake it responds in well under a second.

Not loading? Open it directly at the Space.

How it works

A HGNetV2-B0 backbone predicts over two k-means geo-cell partitions at once, 64 coarse cells and 256 fine ones, blended per image by a learned gate, plus a head that nudges the result off the cell centre.

Four inference methods are selectable in the demo. Medians are measured over the held out test split of 1,764 photos.

What it cannot do

Europe only. The model was trained on 12 countries — Belarus, Finland, France, Germany, Iceland, Italy, Norway, Poland, Spain, Sweden, Turkey and the United Kingdom — and has no class for anywhere else. A photo from outside them will still return one of these twelve, confidently and wrongly.

A median is not a guarantee. Half of all photos land outside the ring drawn around the pin, and the model cannot always tell you which half a given photo is in. The demo flags the cases where its own confidence is low: photos it is that unsure about miss by about 611 km, against 42 km for the rest.

Read the answer as a neighbourhood, not an address.
